CONNECT

Privacy policy and support for the CONNECT app (CONNECT We Create Our Futures).

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Support

Email ahdamwakil0@gmail.com for help, privacy questions, or to request account deletion.

What we collect

  • Account: email address and password.
  • Profile: name, username, photo, job title, company, city or location you type, role, bio, skills, interests, and goals.
  • Contact details you choose to add: email, phone, work phone, and links for LinkedIn, Instagram, X, a website, or a portfolio.
  • Connections, the date you connected, private notes, and contact groups.
  • Network or event membership.

How we use it

We use this information to run your account, show your profile to people at an event, and let you connect with other attendees. Contact details are shown to a connection only when you turn that privacy setting on. We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

Camera, photos, and contacts

The camera is used to scan QR codes and take a profile photo. The photo library is used only so you can choose a profile photo. If you turn on phone-contact sync, CONNECT writes people you connected with into the address book on your device. Your address book is not uploaded.

Who we share it with

Other people in your event can see your public profile. Connections can see the contact details you have allowed. The app is hosted with Supabase, which stores account and profile data to provide the service. We do not share your information with advertisers.

Retention and deletion

We keep your account until you delete it. Email ahdamwakil0@gmail.com and we will delete your account and profile.
